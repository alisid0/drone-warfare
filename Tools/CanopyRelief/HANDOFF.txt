Integration

The branch contains forest code, shaders, one additional crown data texture,
and the review harness. Ground.cs is unchanged, as requested. Apply the exact
Tools/CanopyRelief/ground-integration.patch when integrating. It compiles in a
temporary Ground.patched.cs for candidate harness runs. The after captures
include that proposed floor change; merging the branch alone does not apply it.

The Ground patch removes Varrow's independently culled decorative trees from
the forest-floor inputs and changes litter to shadowed olive (49,70,45), with
the matching linear LitterMean for existing consumers. It preserves occupancy
and all rendered trees. HasFloorCoverage validates exact logical stems, including
candidates that round onto an adjacent hashing cell at large world coordinates.
The patch currently passes git apply --check.

What changed and why

* Age uses a warped 64 m lattice, giving correlated stands roughly 40-120 m
  across. Interior mean tree height spans 12-24 m (18 +/-6 m), and the canopy
  surface follows at approximately +/-4.2 m. Older crowns are larger. Individual
  crown radius varies +/-22%, with the existing conservative 2 m edge minimum.
* 12 m crown and 80 m stand colour noise share the existing three material tint
  batches. Species districts retain their soft 50 m mixing boundary. The glade
  planting recipe, lean, thinning and accepted stem positions are unchanged.
* The same age/cover field drives HLOD geometry, finite-difference normals and
  its 16/32 m macro colour/glade map. Prepared world surfaces start at 32 m;
  streamed bootstrap geometry and its macro map start at 64 m with normals
  derived from the sampled heights. Fine rims retain their stitched boundaries.
* Two independent 512-square crown data maps receive world-block rotations and
  offsets. Three smooth triangular weights blend blocks without visible tile
  edges; transformed normals rotate back into world space. Raster colour now
  uses three detail samples, versus two before. Explicit gradients avoid mip
  seams when the selected block changes. No per-frame texture generation.
* Procedural glades retain dark low understorey, including through the close
  canopy fade. Ordinary grove rims fade normally; the macro alpha distinguishes
  glades from those rims. Recipe woods get low support under their actual crown
  footprints where a narrow strip falls between surface nodes. This support is
  capped at 2.5 m above the floor to avoid opaque walls at grove edges. No extra
  tree instances.
* Reduced trees hand over at 15 projected pixels and cards at 4, before budget
  pressure. SelectProjected reserves one third of each caller's triangle limit
  and one fifth of its card limit for the richer canopy. Existing full/reduced
  count caps, fast-flight deferral and feed-specific budgets still apply.

Evidence

Open Review/index.html for the paired views. The full-resolution PNGs include
Deep Forest and Border Line at 100, 300 and 2,500 m, plus Varrow base, bridge
and farmland at the hd153 strategy camera poses after 0.5 simulated seconds.
The forest cameras are that height above the target, at a 45-degree pitch;
slant distance is sqrt(2) times height. The 2 km crop boundary is an export edge.

These are Blender harness views of production-selected roots and canopy meshes,
using repository FBXs and a replay of the detail shader. Lighting, floor shading
and midpoint fades are approximations. They omit settlement/mission scenery
and HDRP temporal rendering. They are not Unity screenshots or GPU benchmarks.

Review/measurements.json contains every before/after measurement. Baseline code
comes from git object 0542d98 without switching this checkout. Logical counts
match exactly: Deep Forest 104,889; Border Line 104,462; Varrow 9,066.
The sampled glade interior fraction is 8.93% (65,536 samples). Age change over
96 m is about seven times its change over 8 m. All 224 tested dense Varrow
patches have startup canopy; 207 decorative inputs are omitted from its floor.

The flight harness runs two cameras at 240 m/s with the feed every other frame.
Its report includes peak combined tree + canopy triangles, not only trees.
All five candidate routes reduce that combined peak and start no new fine
refinements during fast flight. See the JSON for final counts and CPU p95.
Final peak combined triangles and managed forest CPU p95 (before -> after):
  Deep Forest:     591,429 -> 459,696; 0.420 -> 0.629 ms
  Border Line:     664,223 -> 489,262; 0.431 -> 0.596 ms
  Varrow base:     548,383 -> 443,526; 0.408 -> 0.416 ms
  Varrow bridge:   569,447 -> 447,814; 0.532 -> 0.520 ms
  Varrow farmland: 590,327 -> 426,534; 0.772 -> 0.474 ms
Managed CPU did increase in the two dense sectors; this is not a claim of a
whole-frame performance improvement. Streaming slice completion also affects
which cached surfaces are present, so native controlled comparison is required.
Varrow routes cross open land, so both versions have some zero-tree frames.
Coarse coverage remains present independently of individual tree submission.

Validation completed

* C# 9 forest/terrain integration compiled against Unity 6000.6.7f1 assemblies,
  with the proposed Ground patch, zero warnings and zero errors. Unity was not
  opened. Unchanged HDRP water types use the existing harness signature stubs.
* D3DCompile compiled the new HLSL helpers with warnings as errors, both raster
  and derivative-free paths. The complete HDRP pass/variant build is still needed.
* Production-source checks cover glade fraction, age correlation, exact floor
  ownership, conservative crown radius, startup coverage, raised-edge stitching,
  camera jumps, fast-flight selection budgets and combined geometry regressions.

Reproduce sequentially

Use Python 3.13 with -I for Tools/CanopyRelief/prepare.py, check_shader.py,
run_all.py, audit.py and render_all.py, in that order. run_all accepts a subset
of sector names; render_all accepts 'after' to refresh only candidate captures.
make_detail.py regenerates the alternate procedural crown map. render_all uses
one hidden Blender process at a time with two threads and worktree-local caches.
All assemblies, generated scenes and logs stay in ignored Temp/canopy-relief.
