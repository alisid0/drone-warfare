Changes and evidence

Varrow's logical stems now populate a small index of occupied render cells with
cached bounds. Visible occupied cells load first, directly from those stems,
instead of waiting behind empty world candidate cells. Boundary cells still
include world stems outside the crop. The recipe crop gets a 4 m canopy before
the floor is painted; the previous 128 m grid could miss whole small woods or
interpolate their surface below the ground. Its surrounding world remains coarse.
The completed local surface is retained while viewing the crop instead of being
rebuilt for a world refinement request.

Ground.cs needs NO change. Logical woods remain the input to PaintForestFloor,
Undergrowth and Canopy. This fixes their render coverage rather than deleting
their floor or gameplay occupancy. World.BuildWoods also remains unchanged.

In the production-source harness at Varrow's actual snapped world origin:
  Raised startup canopy in dense 50 m patches: 130/224 before, 224/224 after.
  After 0.5 simulated seconds at the base: 14/26 wooded screen bins contain
    individual crowns before, 26/26 after (154 -> 362 submitted visible roots).
  At the bridge: 12/17 -> 17/17 bins (156 -> 226 submitted visible roots).
  Logical Varrow stems: 9,066 both revisions; none were removed to hide litter.

Cost: recipe-sector construction measured 0.276 -> 1.161 seconds in the managed
harness. The four exported startup blocks contain 303,952 vertices
and 92,632 triangles, versus 5,236 vertices / 3,715 triangles before. The retained
CPU mesh channels are about 13 MiB. Native upload latency and memory need checking.
World-sector interiors retain their existing resolution; mixed rims refine to 8 m.

Fast flight now holds its motion state for 0.5 seconds across skipped feed frames.
Either camera can keep shared canopy refinement deferred. Missing coverage still
preempts fine work, with a 0.20 ms coarse-build slice; stopped views retain 0.85 ms.
At >80 m/s, the main-camera allowances are:
  Below 80 m AGL: full 64, reduced 192, cards 1,800, triangles 650,000.
  Above 80 m AGL: full 32, reduced 160, cards 1,800, triangles 450,000.
  Small feed (<=360 pixels high): 16 / 60 / 900 / 180,000, even when stationary.
  Normal strategy view: existing 200 / 1,000 / 5,000 / 1,800,000 allowances.
Both sides of the existing fades count. Only full trees cast shadows. Instanced
reduced trees already had shadows off; Art now also turns off decorative reduced
tree LOD shadows (riverbanks, gardens and orchards).

The hd151 forest-flight log is sampled after the Swift stops and the camera
zooms out, so its zero tree counts and 0.85 ms build slice do not describe the
whole flight. New interval counters survive that stop and are reset by the
existing FarScatter.ResetTimings call. Mesh and shadow submissions are smaller;
their effect on GPU execution time needs native profiling.

Two-camera 240 m/s harness results, 360 frames, with the feed every other frame:
                      baseline CPU p95   candidate CPU p95
  Varrow base route       0.863 ms            0.717 ms
  Varrow bridge route     1.181 ms            0.705 ms
  Deep Forest route       0.917 ms            0.565 ms
Deep Forest main-view peak tree triangles fell from 1,635,000 to 532,856.
All candidate routes recognized fast movement for 360 frames, started zero
refinements and cancelled zero or one selection. The Deep Forest route discarded
one old deferred canopy build after moving more than 1,024 m, with no replacement
fine build during flight. Canopy build mean fell from about
0.26-0.27 ms to about 0.003 ms. Deep Forest kept individual trees throughout.
Varrow routes cross open land and have zero-tree intervals in BOTH revisions;
the continuous canopy coverage path stays available there.

Reserved glades now have a deterministic 0-8 m irregular margin and a 24 m soft
transition through the existing young edge trees. The reserved interior remains
clear. World procedural glades already had a warped soft recipe, but their
16/32 m canopy mesh cut straight across that margin. Mixed edges now use 8 m
geometry; interior tiles keep the original spacing. Both sides agree on a shared
edge spacing across adaptive tiles, and the block perimeter stays at 8 m. The
harness checks every raised interior mesh edge has exactly two incident faces.
The settled Deep Forest export has 90,068 canopy triangles versus 36,686 before;
this extra simple geometry is substantially smaller than the fast-flight tree
triangle reduction. Fine rim construction is deferred during fast flight too.
The old Blender review also assigned litter with a hard threshold per 8 m triangle, creating
artificial stair steps; both new comparison renders use the same filtered floor.

Verification and limits

Three C# 9 projects compile against Unity 6000.6.7f1 engine assemblies with zero
warnings/errors: forest consumers, complete current terrain integration, and Art.
Unchanged HDRP service signatures have harness stubs; actual changed Renderer,
LODGroup, mesh, instancing and camera APIs use Unity assemblies. The harness checks
startup coverage, canopy seam topology, per-view budgets, two-camera motion,
bounded refinements, strategy flight, projection and a 2.5 km jump. Source is CRLF, deterministic, and
adds no packages or LINQ. Protected gameplay, Ground, water and Codex B files are
unchanged. No Unity Editor or player was launched.

The no-world preview path also passes. Its different seed/origin gives 218/219
raised-canopy patch samples; the remaining sparse rim's exact logical tree was
verified in the submitted batches after moving the camera to it. Live Varrow
retains the stricter 224/224 canopy assertion and complete screen-bin coverage.

Native frame p95 <=12 ms is NOT verified. Managed timings exclude native uploads,
GPU work and the rest of the game. Material/shader behavior and the existing
zero-fallback result must be checked by Claude in the player.
