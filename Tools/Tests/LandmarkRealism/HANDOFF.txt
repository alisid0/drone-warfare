The feature variant seed owns all choices. Inventory RNG rolls are separate from
surface decoration so old tank and wagon counts do not inflate accidentally.
WorldFeatureMap clones polygon outlines into worker snapshots. Sector slabs,
streamed colour/control maps and the world painter use those same surface rules.
Overlapping slabs are cut into disjoint polygons to prevent distant z fighting.
The original graded reserve remains clear, as required by LandmarkCheck. Its
coarse pasture cover is retained; irregular soil tongues break up the visible
yard edge but do not replace that outer cover classification.

Three new FBXs have direct-child LOD meshes, stable metadata and three shared
colours: FreightGroup (828/108/24 triangles), UtilityVehicles (792/344/192),
AggregatePile (236/80/44). Only these small new assets are CPU-readable. Runtime
equipment batches use LOD1/2, share materials by colour, cast no shadows and
crossfade around 150 m before culling at 275 m (normal FOV and lodBias=1).
Pipes, cranes and wagon models receive the same short terminal fade. Distant
HLOD retains buildings, tanks, cooling tower/stacks, surfaces and rail ballast;
equipment, vehicles, piles, pipes and wagons do not enter the distant mesh.

Picture paint investigation
---------------------------
WorldPixels(4096) was run on Unity 6000.6.7f1's shipped Mono JIT, with fresh
snapshots and whole-world first-touch district construction, then warm repeats.
The world-picture path does not call Mathf.PerlinNoise: native roughness is in
the separate height/detail path. Field colour/noise on this path is managed.
Colour encoding previously performed roughly 50 million Math.Pow transfers per
picture, plus repeated transfers of constant road/hedge colours. Encoding now
uses a 64 KiB immutable table; those constant colours are decoded once. A million
uniform probes bound the transfer difference to one byte per channel.

Three-run median cold paint (snapshot included): 6616 -> 5311 ms.
Three-run median warm paint: 5112 -> 3917 ms.
Median process CPU time: cold 90578 -> 69641 ms; warm 63297 -> 56688 ms.
Raw ranges are wide: cold 6266-12655 -> 4745-11550 ms. An after snapshot alone
spiked to 796 ms versus 83-89 ms in its other runs. Host contention is material;
these are indicative savings, not proof of the requested +0.5 s Unity bound or
an explanation of the entire hd139-to-hd143 difference. Raw samples are in
Review/picture-mono-before.json and picture-mono-after.json. The baseline and
after runs were sequential, with no other harness/render launched by this task.
