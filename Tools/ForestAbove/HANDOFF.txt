What changed and why

FarScatter now measures crown diameter in vertical render pixels using the camera
projection and each stem's actual radius. Nominal thresholds are 40/12/3 px.
Per-crown frustum tests prevent invisible trees in large cells from consuming the
visible budget. Full/reduced/card caps remain 200/1000/5000, with 1.8M submitted
triangles including fade overlap. Only full meshes cast shadows. The 3600 m value
is a bounded streaming-cache extent, not a distance LOD. Earlier distance-selector
APIs in ForestBudget remain only for the older offline comparison tools.

Selection and shader projection stay together while new batches are built. A
completed-cell radius and the selection's frustum constrain canopy removal; a
camera jump cannot reuse an old hole. Cached crowns remain visible during normal
movement. Tree reach in the old log now reports the farthest submitted tree;
TierPixels reports the effective thresholds after budget pressure. In dense
Deep Forest the measured thresholds were 40/29.4/17.2 px; Varrow achieved
40/12.05/3 px. Larger dense stands necessarily use the shared canopy detail beyond
the card allowance. No GPU evidence was available to justify increasing that cap.

The canopy was at 0.80 of stand height while broadleaf photo caps are at 0.72.
That hid arriving crowns behind the sheet. The stand surface is now at 0.55,
retaining the existing stand/cluster relief and normals. A single 512-square
linear, mipmapped texture supplies photo-derived crown light and normal slopes,
including on bootstrap blocks. Its 8 m crowns and second 21 m sampling scale give
both crown and stand variation. Species still blend through ForestPalette.
Mature-stand linear luminance versus the 105/126/86 reference pasture measures
0.626/0.675 for broadleaf and 0.427/0.462 for conifers, before illumination/fog.
The new texture occupies about 1.33 MiB on the GPU with uncompressed mipmaps.

Refinement now uses 16/32 m nearby geometry with the existing shared 8 m seams;
small species maps replace the old 260-square crown bakes. The shared texture
supplies detail at all levels. Stationary building gets a bounded 0.85 ms slice;
movement above 80 m/s retains the prior 0.30 ms slice. This matters because the
native 4x Swift flight moves about 240 m/s. Missing coverage still preempts fine
work. CoarseBlocks now includes any under-resolved visible block, not just step
256; NearCoarseBlocks counts those wanted at 16/32 m, including outdated focus.

World.BuildWoods already called SectorForest for both world and recipe maps.
The non-world regression was in PlanGroves: the replacement had lost the old
inner/outer quotas and sampled the whole sector. It could place all useful woods
away from the base. The logical generator again allocates inner and outer groves,
finishing the inner quota first, with the existing road/base/field exclusions.
World.cs did not need an edit. The distance-tier problem also hid Varrow's
remaining individual trees from the strategy camera.

The reduced pine FBX contains BarkPine.001, verified by importing all eight
full/reduced forest FBXs in headless Blender. Only BarkPine.mat exists in Forest.
Its three tint clones account for the three missing material lookups. Known
Blender numeric suffixes now resolve only when the canonical serialized forest
asset exists. One remap message names that asset. Any actual fallback logs the
original material, tier and observed cause: forced test, missing resource, missing
shader, unsupported shader, serialized shader mismatch, missing property, or
failed material/property-block readback. LastFallback retains that reason.

Reproduce locally, one process at a time

Use C:/Users/ali10/AppData/Local/Programs/Python/Python313/python.exe -I for:
  Tools/ForestAbove/prepare.py
  Tools/ForestAbove/run.py before deep
  Tools/ForestAbove/run.py before varrow
  Tools/ForestAbove/run.py after deep
  Tools/ForestAbove/run.py after varrow

checkout. Generated scene/mesh data stays under ignored Temp/forest-above.
Tools/ForestAbove/make_detail.py rebuilds the committed shared texture using the
existing photo-derived crown stamps. It requires only Python's standard library.

For each of "before deep", "after deep", "before varrow", "after varrow":
  "C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" --background --factory-startup --threads 2 --python Tools/ForestAbove/render.py -- <phase> <sector>

Do not run these renders concurrently with each other or a Unity build. Observed
Blender working sets were roughly 0.9-1.0 GB; no Library import is needed.
